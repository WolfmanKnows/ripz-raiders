How to Play

• You start with a Hootling companion.

• Talk to people on the pack floor. Some look friendly...

• ...until they get exposed as scalpers trying to buy out the stock.

• Challenge them: "You're not who you said you were! You're trying to buy out Walmart!"

• Battle their bootleg creatures with yours.

• Win → You save the stock for the kids.

• Lose → The scalper clears the shelves.
